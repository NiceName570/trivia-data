AWARDS
The Silence of the Lambs (1991) won the Big Five at the 64th Academy Awards (Mar 30, 1992): Picture, Director (Jonathan Demme), Actor (Anthony Hopkins), Actress (Jodie Foster), Adapted Screenplay (Ted Tally).
Kathy Bates won Best Actress as Annie Wilkes in Misery (1990) at the 63rd Academy Awards (Mar 25, 1991) - the film's only nomination. She also won the Golden Globe for Best Actress - Drama.
Bram Stoker's Dracula (1992) won 3 of 4 Oscars at the 65th Academy Awards (1993): Costume Design (Eiko Ishioka), Makeup (Greg Cannom et al.), Sound Effects Editing; lost Art Direction.
Sleepy Hollow (1999) won Best Art Direction (Rick Heinrichs, Peter Young) at the 72nd Academy Awards (Mar 26, 2000); also nominated for Cinematography and Costume Design (Colleen Atwood).
The Sixth Sense (1999) got 6 Oscar nominations, 0 wins: Picture; Director and Original Screenplay (M. Night Shyamalan); Supp. Actor (Haley Joel Osment); Supp. Actress (Toni Collette); Film Editing.
BOX OFFICE AND RECORDS
The Sixth Sense (1999) became the highest-grossing horror film ever, passing The Exorcist, and held the record until It (2017), which reached about $704M.
Scream grossed $173M worldwide ($103M US). It was the highest-grossing slasher film until Halloween (2018), and Miramax's second $100M US film after Pulp Fiction.
The Blair Witch Project grossed $248.6M worldwide ($140.5M US/Canada) after a tiny budget; 10th-highest-grossing US film of 1999.
Halloween H20 was the highest-grossing Halloween film until Halloween (2018); Bride of Chucky is the highest-grossing Chucky film.
DEBUTS AND CASTING
Leonardo DiCaprio made his film debut as Josh in Critters 3 (1991).
Jennifer Aniston's first major film role was Tory Redding in Leprechaun (1993); she earlier had an uncredited bit in Mac and Me (1988).
Paul Rudd made his feature film debut in Halloween: The Curse of Michael Myers (credited as Paul Stephen Rudd), playing Tommy Doyle.
Josh Hartnett made his film debut in Halloween H20 and also starred in The Faculty the same year.
Drew Barrymore was first cast as Sidney in Scream but asked for the smaller role of Casey Becker, who dies in the opening.
Charlize Theron made her film debut in an uncredited, non-speaking role in Children of the Corn III: Urban Harvest (1995).
BEHIND THE SCENES
Brandon Lee was killed on The Crow set on Mar 31, 1993 (EUE Screen Gems, Wilmington, NC) by a bullet lodged in a prop .44 revolver (squib load).
Candyman used over 200,000 real honeybees; Tony Todd negotiated a $1,000 bonus per sting and was stung 23 times.
The Ghostface mask in Scream was based on Fun World's 1991 'Peanut-Eyed Ghost' costume design.

INTERNATIONAL HORROR
Hideo Nakata's Ring (1998, Japan) launched the J-horror boom and was remade in English as The Ring (2002).
Braindead (1992), Peter Jackson's New Zealand zombie splatter comedy set in Wellington, was released in the US as Dead Alive.
